THE PROBLEM

Three mechanisms are quietly compounding your electricity cost

None of them show up as a single alarming line item. All three show up on every invoice, and two of them carry forward for months after the event that caused them.

Demand ratchet
A single short peak sets your billed demand for the rest of the month — in some networks, for up to 12 months forward. One bad afternoon becomes a year-long cost.
TARIFF: $/kVA/MONTH
RATCHET WINDOW: UP TO 12MO
Power factor penalty
Motors and drives that draw reactive power get penalised directly on the bill, independent of how much real work that power did.
PENALTY: REACTIVE kVAr
TRIGGER: LOW COS φ
Harmonic distortion
VFDs and soft starters inject harmonics back into the network. Left unmanaged, it's a compliance exposure as much as a cost one.
STANDARD: AS/NZS 61000
SOURCE: DIODE-RECTIFIER VFDs
